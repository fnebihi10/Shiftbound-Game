# Opening asset and material specifications

This describes the implemented benchmark candidate, not a purchase list or final art approval. Assets live under `UnityProject/Assets/Shiftbound/ProductionBenchmark/`. All dimensions are metres; gameplay colliders remain the originals.

| Asset | Implemented specification | Production limitation |
|---|---|---|
| WindowBay prefab | 3.0 × 3.1 m nominal bay; 1.36 × 1.74 m aperture; 0.18 m reveal; 65 mm perimeter frames; 40 mm central mullion; 100 mm sill projecting 0.32 m. Three material slots: masonry, painted metal, opaque cool glass. | Needs art review of repetition, weathering, frame detailing and window variation. Glass is opaque PBR, not a simulated interior. |
| Constructed facades | Window bays fill all four exterior faces; dimensions match existing building masses. Each building is one saved mesh with three submeshes. Top cap completes orbit coverage. No new collision. | No authored room interiors, trims/doors/roof drainage kit or LOD family. Far buildings still need visual consistency review. |
| ServiceUnit prefab | 1.30 m wide, 1.10 m main casing, ~1.4 m depth; 35 mm vertical corner chamfer; recessed front grille with six tilted louvers; overhanging zinc cap. Graphite and ochre variants share the mesh. Landing instance is scaled to existing obstacle. | Purposefully consolidates earlier overlapping shells. It is not a certified hero HVAC asset; needs material wear, construction detail and artist approval. |
| Roof slabs | Original geometry/transforms and colliders. UVs represent 0.5 repeat/m, one source texture repeat per 2 m. Shared opening slabs use the same concrete in both states. | Existing concrete source resembles rough aggregate more than the concept's constructed paving; needs a licensed/commissioned paving material with physical reference. |
| Hanging ivy | Generated 1024×1536 RGBA image with real transparent pixels; lit URP alpha test, cutoff 0.45, two-sided, no transparency blend. Five attached opening cards. | Cards show planar silhouettes during orbit. Not a replacement for rooted 3D stems, canopy volumes, LODs or wind-tested vegetation. |
| World panoramas | Two generated 1774×887 2:1 raster textures, same referenced city, imported with mipmaps and high-quality compression. `Skybox/Panoramic`, lat-long mapping, 90° rotation, exposure 1. No world-space matte rectangle. | This is ~1.6 MP, not 4K/8K despite a high-resolution generation request. Soft angular detail, edge/projection artifacts and 2D motion remain below the production target. Matching landmark appearance is visually reviewed, not mathematically guaranteed pixel registration. |
| Courier | Existing Quaternius humanoid and five actual runtime states. Jog/Sprint transitions preserve normalized cycle phase. Root motion stays off. | Proportions, clothing, backpack shape and movement are still stand-in quality. No premium substitute was fabricated. |

URP Lit materials retain the licensed Poly Haven diffuse/normal sources. Facade smoothness 0.12, normal strength 0.4; concrete smoothness 0.12. Painted window frames: metallic 0.15, smoothness 0.35. Opaque glass: metallic 0.15, smoothness 0.7. Equipment: metallic 0.45/smoothness 0.35; ochre 0.2/0.3; zinc 0.65/0.4. These are editable candidate values, not measured physical reflectance. Masonry and roof UVs use a 2 m repeat; material tiling is 1.

Lighting: original sun orientation 35° elevation / -44° yaw; intensity 1.65; shadow strength 0.85. Present sunlight RGB (1, .91, .76); Overgrown (1, .92, .80). Trilight sky RGB (.43, .53, .65), equator 60% and ground 28% of the target ambient color. Linear fog 70–235 m. ACES; bloom intensity .08/threshold 1.3; contrast +3/saturation -4. No new lights or post-effect stack complexity.

Preview surfaces use a separate transparent shader: no depth write, faint sides, world-space diagonal top hatching and object-space perimeter outline. WorldSwitcher retains full original material-slot arrays and hides non-colliding inactive decoration. Originally disabled renderers stay disabled. The blocked Shift test wall remains an actual solid collider; its conspicuous masonry block is an unresolved art problem.

## Required external assets, in priority order

1. **Courier:** recognizable young adult with credible anatomy, yellow/orange outerwear, cyan accent, fitted trousers/shoes, authored hair and integrated backpack. Unity Humanoid-compatible rig, clean skinning, hand/shoulder/backpack clearance, licensed redistribution, source files and PC LODs. Validate at the actual 7.6 m / 60° gameplay camera before purchase approval.
2. **Motion set:** compatible in-place idle, walk/jog/run, acceleration, stop, 90°/180° turns, jump takeoff, rising/falling poses and landings. Require foot contacts, gait phase consistency, root orientation and pose continuity; separate pose timing from motor physics. Include backpack secondary motion only if it survives gameplay and performance verification.
3. **Near rooftop kit/materials:** authored concrete/limestone coping, flashing, drains, believable worn service equipment and paving. Consistent metre dimensions, PBR textures with normal/roughness provenance, reusable corners and plausible construction. Replace this candidate kit where independent artist review rejects it.
4. **Vegetation:** rooted ivy with stem attachment, varied silhouettes, leaf cards/volumes, two-sided normal handling and LOD/alpha-overdraw budgets. Growth must be composed around real edges and drainage rather than covering route markers.
5. **Far-city art:** genuinely seamless 360° spherical city/sky artwork at sufficient angular resolution (evaluate 8K as a candidate, then measure memory and bandwidth), registered Present/Overgrown landmarks; optional limited 3D midground only where it measurably improves motion. No expansion of the explorable city is implied.
