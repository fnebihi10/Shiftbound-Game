# V4 generated bitmap assets

These project assets were created with the built-in image-generation tool and copied into `UnityProject/Assets/Shiftbound/TexturesV4/`. They are visual layers, not generated 3D models.

## Skyline_Overgrown.png

Reference: `ArtDirection/visual-target-gameview-v2.png` (style and composition reference).

Prompt: Create a wide 16:9 distant overgrown city skyline at golden late afternoon, viewed horizontally from rooftop height. Use varied sandstone and concrete towers with rooftop gardens and vine silhouettes, a broad stone viaduct in the right middle distance, mist between city layers, believable blue sky, soft cream clouds, and a low sun near the right horizon. Keep the lower quarter mostly mist and far silhouettes for real 3D rooftops in front. No people, nearby rooftops, platforms, UI, writing, or logos.

## Skyline_Present.png

Reference: `Skyline_Overgrown.png` (edit target).

Prompt: Create the present-world counterpart while preserving the camera framing, horizon, tower positions and silhouettes, viaduct, mountains, clouds, and right-side sun. Change the overgrown city into a maintained city with selective urban greenery, cooler late-afternoon color, and the same layered stylized game-art quality. No people, near rooftops, gameplay platforms, UI, writing, or logos.

## Ivy_Hanging.png

Prompt: Create one hanging spray of varied ivy leaves and tendrils growing from the top, in a detailed stylized 3D game-art look under warm late-afternoon light. Keep the entire plant within the frame on a genuinely transparent alpha background. No wall, planter, sky, text, border, or background shadow. This is an alpha cutout on a 3D quad.

The paired city images are attached to a distant camera-facing quad in V4. Real gameplay surfaces, collisions, and nearby buildings remain 3D. The approval image is a concept reference, not a playable render.
